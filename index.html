How to play

  1. Goal: make every ring hold orbs of a single color.
  2. Spin: tap a ring to turn it one slot clockwise, or drag it around in either direction. Mouse wheel works too.
  3. Swap: tap a bridge ⇄ to exchange the two orbs it connects.
  4. Moves are limited: every slot of spin and every swap costs one move. Undo gives moves back.
  5. Stars: match par (the fewest possible) for three stars. A new ★★★ or a daily puzzle earns a hint.
  6. Mechanisms: anchored rings (padlock) never spin, one-way rings (arrows) only turn clockwise, geared rings (cog) turn together in opposite directions, and star charts (colored rims) must match their pattern exactly.

Keyboard: Z undo · R restart · H hint · Esc back
